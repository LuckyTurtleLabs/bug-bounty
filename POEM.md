# Work Between the Braces

In FreelanceFlow, a brief becomes a door:\
a client names the thing they cannot build.\
A maker reads, then asks one question more;\
the blank proposal waits until it is filled.

Through routes and schemas, small intentions pass:\
a message, then a milestone given shape.\
Two distant desks look through the browser glass;\
an idea finds a working first escape.

One test turns red where hurried eyes saw green;\
we trace the fault and leave a smaller diff.\
SecureBananaLabs keeps room between\
the work we trust and one more careful "if."
